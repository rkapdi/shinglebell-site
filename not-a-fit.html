Our system is built for roofing companies with enough call volume that it pays for itself many times over. Under $1M a year, it usually doesn't, and we'd rather tell you now than take your money.

You can still stop most missed calls yourself this week, for close to nothing. Here's the checklist we'd follow.

The missed-call checklist

  • Count your misses for 7 days.Open your phone's call log each evening and write down missed calls from numbers you don't know. That's your baseline.
  • Turn on a missed-call text.Most business phone apps can text a missed caller automatically: "Sorry we missed you, we're on a roof. What do you need?" A text gets answered when a voicemail doesn't.
  • Re-record your voicemail greeting.Say your company name, that you're on a job, when you'll call back, and that they can text this number. Keep it under 15 seconds.
  • Set two callback windows a day.Lunch and end of day, every day. Call back every miss in that window, newest first.
  • Forward the line when you can't answer.On install days, forward calls to someone who can pick up: your office, a partner, a family member with a short script.
  • Fix your hours on Google.If your Google Business Profile says you're open until 8 and nobody answers at 7, change one or the other.
  • Put a text or form option on your site.Some people won't call at all. Give them a way to ask for an estimate, and make sure it goes to a phone you check.
  • Follow up every quote twice.Two days after you send it and one week after. A calendar reminder is enough.
