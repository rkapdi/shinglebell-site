What we collect

When you book a Missed-Jobs Check, you give us your name, email, phone number, company name and website, and the business figures you choose to share (revenue range, average job value, leads per month). The booking is handled by Cal.com, which stores it on our behalf.

To run the check we call your public business phone number, submit your public website form, and look at your public Google reviews. We take notes on what happened. We do not record the test calls.

How we use it

To run your check, prepare your report, hold the review call, and follow up with you about it. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your check results in our marketing unless you agree in writing.

Advertising and analytics

This site uses the Meta pixel to measure whether our ads lead to booked checks. Meta may use cookies to do this. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram settings and through your browser's cookie controls.
